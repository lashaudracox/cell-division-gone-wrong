Key Genes
MSH2 · Mismatch repair

Caretaker. Proofreads newly copied DNA after S phase and fixes mismatched bases.

BRCA2 · Homologous recombination

Caretaker. Repairs breaks through both DNA strands using the matching chromosome.

XP genes · Excision repair

Caretakers. Cut out bulky damage such as UV kinks and fill in the gap.

TP53 · Gatekeeper

If damage gets past repair, p53 can trigger apoptosis. One working copy is enough to keep this protection.

NRAS · Proto-oncogene

One activating mutation locks it ON, sending a nonstop "divide" signal.

How this model works
  • Each division: 1 copying error, plus a 30% chance of a DNA break and a 30% chance of UV damage.
  • A working repair system fixes its kind of damage 95% of the time. A broken one fixes none.
  • If damage isn't repaired and at least one TP53 copy works, p53 removes the cell 35% of the time. A healthy sister cell carries on.
  • Otherwise the damage becomes a permanent mutation. 1 in 5 mutations hits a driver gene (TP53 copy 1, TP53 copy 2, or NRAS).
  • Real rates are far lower per division, but real tissues have billions of cells dividing for years.
