📋 Stage-by-Stage Reference
#What happensGermline or somatic?Where doctors find it
①Maya inherits one broken TP53 copy in her mother's egg.GermlineEvery cell, so it shows up in the skin sample
②As Maya grows, every new cell copies that first hit.GermlineSkin, blood, saliva: all normal tissue
③One blood-forming cell in her bone marrow loses TP53 copy 2. The brake is gone.SomaticLeukemia cells only
④The same cell's NRAS gene is switched on. One hit is enough for an oncogene.SomaticLeukemia cells only
⑤The cell divides nonstop. All its descendants make up the leukemia clone.(the result)Bone marrow: 72% of cells were leukemia cells (Lesson 2)
Two-hit check: Maya needed only one TP53 hit after birth because the first was inherited. A child born with two working copies would need two TP53 hits in the same cell, plus NRAS.
