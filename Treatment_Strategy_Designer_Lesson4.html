🧩 Step 3 · Build the Plan

Check every option your team would include, up to five. For each one, write why it fits this patient's biology. Leaving an option unchecked is a decision too, so be ready to defend it.

Simplified for learning. Real treatment decisions are made by a care team with the family and depend on many details not shown here. This is not medical advice.
