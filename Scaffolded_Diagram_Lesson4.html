⑤Spindle fibersVincristine (M phase)Any dividing cell (not specific to one mutation)
⑥p53 → apoptosis(what many chemo drugs rely on)Working TP53. If both copies are lost, chemo may fail.
Pattern to notice: targets ①–③ are specific (they need a particular marker or mutation). Targets ④–⑤ hit any dividing cell, which is why chemo has side effects in hair, gut, and bone marrow.
