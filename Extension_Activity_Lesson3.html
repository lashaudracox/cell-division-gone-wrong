How Often Is Childhood Cancer Inherited?

Analyze real genome-sequencing data from more than 1,000 children with cancer

📖 Background
You found that Maya's first TP53 hit was inherited. How unusual is that? In 2015, a research team ran tumor–normal sequencing on 1,120 children and teens with cancer. They searched the normal-tissue DNA for germline mutations in 60 genes known to raise cancer risk. Their results changed how many hospitals think about genetic testing for children with cancer.
Remember: a germline mutation shows up in normal tissue, so it was present from conception and could be shared with relatives.
